# Personal preferences in job selection

Free-form notes scored by the JD analyzer. Each line that earns points
contributes to `pers_preference_score` in `analysis.json`, which is how you
sort a pile of postings by what *you* actually want rather than by match
percentage alone. Rewrite these for yourself -- the format is just
"preference (N points)".

 - Balance location against compensation.
    - Within 60 km of home: EUR 55k and above (1 point)
    - Capital-city commute: EUR 70k and above (1 point)
    - Anywhere else in the country: EUR 80k and above (1 point)
 - The role is hybrid, or the office is within commuting distance (1 point)
 - The role is fully remote (0.5 points)
 - The role requires a second language I speak (3 points)
 - The company operates in a domain I have shipped in before (1 point)
 - Well-known international company (1 point)
