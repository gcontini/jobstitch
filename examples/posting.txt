Head of IT Infrastructure — Acme Logistics Group
Milan, Italy (hybrid, 2 days on site) · Permanent · Posted 3 days ago

About the role
Acme Logistics Group operates a European distribution network of 14 warehouses
and a fleet of 600 vehicles. We are looking for a Head of IT Infrastructure to
own the platforms that keep that network running: two data centres, a growing
public cloud estate, and the connectivity between them and every site.

You will lead a team of nine engineers across infrastructure, networking and
service desk, and report to the CIO. The role combines hands-on technical
ownership with budget responsibility and vendor management. This is a
consolidation phase, not a greenfield one: much of the first year is rationalising
what a decade of acquisitions has left behind.

What you will do
- Own the availability, performance and cost of all infrastructure services,
  on premise and in the cloud.
- Lead the migration of legacy workloads from our two data centres into a
  hybrid cloud target architecture, retiring at least one facility.
- Define and enforce standards for provisioning, patching, monitoring and
  backup across all sites.
- Run the annual infrastructure budget (approximately EUR 4M) and the
  relationships with our main hardware, connectivity and cloud vendors.
- Build the on-call rotation and incident process, and act as final escalation
  for major incidents.
- Grow the team: hiring, mentoring, and setting technical direction.

What we are looking for
- Eight or more years in infrastructure engineering, with at least three
  leading a team.
- Deep experience of virtualisation and Linux, plus one major public cloud
  (we use AWS, with a small Azure footprint).
- Proven track record of a data centre consolidation or cloud migration
  delivered end to end.
- Comfortable owning a budget and negotiating with vendors.
- Working knowledge of ITIL service management practices.
- Fluent English; Italian is an advantage but not a requirement.

What we offer
- EUR 85,000 – 105,000 depending on experience, plus an annual bonus.
- Hybrid working, two days per week in our Milan office.
- Training budget and certification support.

Acme Logistics Group is an equal opportunity employer.
